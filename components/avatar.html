Avatar

Avatars represent a person or entity with an image or initials. They help users quickly identify who is involved in a record, comment, or assignment. Use consistent sizes and colors so people stay recognizable across the product.

Sizes

Three sizes cover the platform. Medium is the default. Choose a size based on the density of the surrounding context.

Size Class Dimensions Usage
SD
 hb-avatar--sm 24 × 24px Dense tables and chips
SD
 hb-avatar--md 32 × 32px Default — sidebars, cards, list rows
SD
 hb-avatar--lg 40 × 40px Top nav and comment threads

Types & colors

Initials avatars use a soft expressive background paired with the matching contrast color for the text. Assign a consistent color per person so they stay recognizable. A solid variant is available for default or system avatars.

The hb-avatar--muted variant renders the whole avatar — background and initials — at reduced opacity. Use it to de-emphasize avatars, for example every participant except the active one in an AvatarGroup. It works with any --hb-avatar-bg override.

AvatarGroup

Group overlapping avatars to show several people in a compact space. Avatars overlap with a negative margin (Large -16px, Medium -10px, Small -8px) and carry a 2px ring in the background color to keep them visually separated. The ring defaults to --ui-bg (white); when the group sits on another surface, override it on the group with --hb-avatar-ring (e.g. style="--hb-avatar-ring: var(--ui-bg-tertiary)") so it blends in. Show a maximum of 5 avatars and collapse the rest into a +N overflow counter.

Best practices

Do
SD

Use meaningful initials — first and last name.

MR
AC

Keep avatars the same size within a single context.

EC
SD
+6

Collapse to a +N counter once you pass 5 avatars.

Don't
SD

Don't scale avatars outside the sm / md / lg tokens.

MR
AC

Don't mix avatar sizes in the same row or group.

JD

Don't use an avatar as a primary navigation trigger without a clear affordance.
