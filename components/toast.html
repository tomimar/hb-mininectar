Components

Toast

Short-lived notifications that acknowledge a user action or surface a system event. Unlike alerts, toasts are transient — they appear briefly in a corner of the viewport and never block interaction.

Example

A solid-colored bar with an icon, message, optional action button and optional dismiss. Max-width 720px, drop shadow.

Scheduled maintenance starts Sunday at 2am.

<div class="hb-toast hb-toast--info">
  <svg class="hb-toast__icon" …></svg>
  <p class="hb-toast__content">Message</p>
  <button class="hb-toast__action">Undo</button>
  <button class="hb-toast__dismiss">…</button>
</div>

Types

Five variants. Info and Success auto-dismiss; Warning and Error never do. Loading persists until the process ends.

InfoNeutral notices. Auto-dismiss after 4s.

Scheduled maintenance at 2am Sunday.

SuccessConfirms an action. Optionally include "Undo".

Message deleted

WarningBlack text on yellow. Never auto-dismiss; always dismissible.

Your session will expire in 5 minutes. Save your changes.

ErrorA failure. Never auto-dismiss. Optional "Retry".

Your request could not be completed. Please try again.

LoadingOngoing background process. Spinner + optional "Cancel". Never auto-dismiss.

Reading document… This may take a moment.

Toast vs Alert

TriggerAction / background processPage load / system status
PlacementCorner of viewport (overlay)Top of page (in-flow)
LifespanTransient, auto-dismissesPersistent until resolved
Use forFeedback, background tasksSystem-wide status

Best practices

Do

Keep messages to 1–2 lines.

Use only one action link per toast.

Stack toasts vertically (max 3 visible).

Auto-dismiss info and success toasts.

Don't

Auto-dismiss errors or warnings.

Use for blocking errors — use a Modal.

Stack more than 3 toasts at once.

Put long copy, multiple CTAs, or form validation in a toast.
