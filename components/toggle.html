Components

Toggle

Toggles (also known as switches) let users switch a setting between two mutually exclusive states: on and off. Unlike checkboxes, toggles take effect immediately without requiring form submission.

Example

Wrap a hidden checkbox in hb-toggle with a hb-toggle__track (containing the hb-toggle__thumb) and a hb-toggle__label. The control is fully interactive — click it.

<label class="hb-toggle">
  <input type="checkbox" class="hb-toggle__input" checked>
  <span class="hb-toggle__track"><span class="hb-toggle__thumb"></span></span>
  <span class="hb-toggle__label">Notifications</span>
</label>

Best practices

Do

Use toggles for immediate, binary on/off settings.

Place the label to the right of the toggle.

Keep labels short (e.g. "Notifications", "Dark mode").

Ensure the on and off states are always visually distinct.

Don't

Use toggles when changes need confirmation — use Checkbox.

Replace grouped multi-selections with toggles.

Leave a toggle without a clear text label.

Use toggles for destructive actions without confirmation.
