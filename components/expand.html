Components

Expand

The component for showing and hiding content. A button opens and closes a region of content right below it. It covers what people often call an accordion, a collapsible, a collapse card or a show more: one behaviour, one card look, and two patterns built from them.

Overview

There is one behaviour, hb-expand: a native button with aria-expanded and aria-controls, and the region it controls. hb-expand--card gives it the bordered card look. Show more and stacked cards (the "accordion") are patterns built from these, not separate components.

info
Coming from Nectar? This replaces three components:
  • HbExpand → hb-expand (base).
  • HbAccordion → hb-expand--card hb-expand--muted-header. Despite the name it was always a single section.
  • HbCollapseCard → hb-expand--card. Its loading state is the card's loading state; its "composed" variant is the show more pattern.

Base — hb-expand

Behaviour only, no chrome. Put hb-expand__toggle on a button, usually a tertiary Button, and place the region straight after it. The chevron turns over when the region is open. Use it for small bits of secondary detail inside other content.

Card — hb-expand--card

A bordered, rounded card for a self-contained section. The whole header, title and chevron, is one native button placed inside a heading. Examples use h3; pick the level that fits the page outline. The chevron points down when closed and up when open. When the card is open, a divider separates header and content.

A count on its own is fine when the title says what it counts, like Linked accounts 3. Add a word when it counts a subset or something else, like Transactions 12 flagged.

Card with header actions

Actions such as an overflow menu sit next to the toggle button in the header, in hb-expand__actions. They are siblings of the heading, never inside the button. Because they are separate elements, clicking an action doesn't toggle the card, with no extra code. The actions reuse the ghost icon Button and the overflow menu from Table.

check Do
Put header actions in hb-expand__actions, next to the heading.
Name icon-only actions after the section: "More actions for Linked accounts".
close Don't
Put links, buttons or any other interactive element inside the toggle button. Nested click targets are invalid HTML, and keyboard and screen reader users can't reach them reliably.
Fix a nested action with stopPropagation. Move it out of the button instead.

Show more pattern — hb-expand--show-more

The first lines of the content stay visible and the toggle sits at the bottom of the card, in hb-expand__footer. The intent is different from a card header: a card header hides a whole section, while show more lets you keep reading something you've already started. Only use it when the content is clearly longer than the preview.

info
Text only. Don't put links, buttons or other interactive elements in the content. The clipped part is only hidden visually, so they stay focusable while you can't see them. If the content needs them, use a Card instead.
Case note · Elena Cruz · 2 Jul 2026

Subject made 12 cash deposits into personal checking ••4821 between 2 and 28 June 2026, each between $9,400 and $9,900, for a total of $115,200. Deposits were made at three branches, often on the same day, which is consistent with an attempt to avoid currency transaction reporting.

Within 48 hours of each deposit, most of the funds were moved to business checking ••7710 (Okafor Trading LLC) and then wired to Lagos Freight Partners Ltd. The subject's stated occupation is import/export broker, but the account has no history of trade-related payments, invoices or shipping costs before May 2026.

KYC was last refreshed in March 2024 and lists expected monthly cash activity under $2,000. Two previous alerts exist: ALERT-1520 (Sep 2025), closed with a SAR for similar structuring, and ALERT-1874 (Feb 2026), closed with no SAR after the customer provided invoices.

Recommendation: escalate to enhanced due diligence, request source of funds for the June deposits, and prepare a continuing-activity SAR.

Here the toggle comes after the content, not before it: the reader has already started, and the button is where they stop. Collapsing keeps focus on the button and scrolls it back into view.

Several open at onceWe don't use "only one open at a time". Analysts and investigators often compare sections side by side, such as alerts or subjects, and closing one when they open another gets in their way.
Expand allHelps when users need to scan everything, or search the page with the browser's find, which can't see closed sections.
A pattern, not a componentThere is no hb-accordion. This becomes a dedicated component only if three or more real use cases need behaviour beyond this (rule of three).

  • Critical information users must see: risk scores, warnings, required actions.
  • Very short content. Opening it costs more than reading it.

Accessibility

accessibility_new
What every expand needs.
  • A native <button type="button">. Enter and Space work for free, no key handlers needed.
  • aria-expanded on the button, updated on every toggle, and aria-controls pointing to the region's id.
  • For cards, the button sits inside a heading, so screen reader users can jump between sections by heading. Cards also name their region with role="region" and aria-labelledby.
  • No interactive elements inside the toggle. Header actions go next to it.
  • A visible focus state. The card header shows a focus ring inside its edge.
  • The content comes right after its toggle in the DOM, so reading order matches what's on screen. Show more is the one exception: the preview comes first and the button follows it. Its preview must be text only: links or buttons in the clipped part stay focusable while hidden. Use a Card when the content needs them.
  • The chevron is decorative (aria-hidden); the state comes from aria-expanded.
