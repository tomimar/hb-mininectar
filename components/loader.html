Loader

Loaders communicate that a process is underway. Both variants are indeterminate — they animate continuously without tracking specific progress, reducing perceived wait time and signaling that the interface hasn't frozen.

Types

Two variants for two scopes of loading.

Circular20px spinner. Component- or button-level loading.

LinearFull-width 4px bar. Page- or section-level loading.

On dark backgrounds

Add hb-loader--contrast to the circular spinner when it sits on a dark surface (e.g. a loading Toast).

Best practices

Do

Use linear for page- or section-level loading.

Use circular for component- or button-level loading.

Unmount the loader as soon as content is ready.

Pair with a loading Toast for user-triggered background tasks.

Don't

Use linear inside a button or small element.

Use circular as a full-page loader.

Show both variants for the same action.

Use a loader for operations under ~300ms (causes flicker).
